Capture what matters, instantly
Photo or voice — Pillier turns a 30-minute paper report into a 60-second AI-analyzed incident record. Description, probable cause, recommended solution, and applicable norms — all generated automatically.
Build institutional memory
Every incident captured becomes searchable knowledge. After 90 days, Pillier identifies patterns humans miss — recurring categories, high-risk trades, projects that need attention. Your next project starts smarter than your last.
Stay compliant, stay protected
Every record is timestamped, geotagged, and audit-ready. Pillier automatically references applicable norms (ABNT NBR, NRs, DTU) — so when inspectors or lawyers come knocking, your documentation speaks for itself.

Industries
Civil construction
Residential and commercial builds, from single-family homes to high-rise developments. Foundation, structural, quality control and safety captures.
Architecture
Track how designs are executed on site, document deviations, and maintain a visual record of the built reality vs. the planned design.
Structural engineering
Cracks, deflections, settlement — captured, referenced against NBR 6118 or Eurocode 2, and turned into defensible records.
Infrastructure & public works
Roads, bridges, tunnels, water systems. Timestamped, geotagged, norm-referenced records that hold up in audits and tenders.
Renovation & retrofit
Document what plans do not show: hidden pipes, unexpected structural conditions, materials that no longer meet current codes.
Real estate development
Portfolio-scale visibility across multiple sites. Cross-project pattern detection and early warning on troubled projects.
